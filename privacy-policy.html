Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Preamble

This Privacy Policy describes how the Savevia mobile application ("the Application"), developed by Benashe Softworks ("the Studio"), collects, uses and protects user information. By using Savevia, you agree to the practices described in this document.

Savevia is a free, ad-supported Android application that allows downloading publicly available media from popular social media platforms, saving temporary status or story content shared via messaging applications, converting video files into audio files, converting photos into PDF documents, compressing PDF files, compressing video files, generating AI-powered summaries of YouTube videos, and securely storing personal files in a local, PIN or password-protected Vault.

Article 1 — Data Controller Identity

Studio: Benashe Softworks
Contact email: benashesoftworks@gmail.com
Capacity: Independent studio, sole data controller for the Savevia Application.

Article 2 — Data Collected

2.1 No collection of personal data

Savevia does not collect any personally identifiable data. No account, name, email or phone number is required.

2.2 Anonymized technical data

Anonymized technical data may be collected by integrated third-party services (e.g. Firebase Crashlytics) for performance purposes. Advertising-related data processed by Google AdMob is described in Article 7.

2.3 No In-App Purchases, Subscriptions or Payment Data

Savevia is entirely free. It offers no paid tier, no subscription and no in-app purchase of any kind.

Savevia does not collect, process or store any payment or financial information, as no purchase is ever required or offered.

2.4 Local advertising-frequency data

To avoid showing too many advertisements, the Application keeps a few counters on the device only: the number of actions performed and of full-screen ads shown during the day, the time of the last full-screen ad and the start of the current session. These counters are never transmitted, are not linked to any identity, and are deleted when the Application is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Article 3 — Access to Device Media Folders

3.1 Saving status/story content

Savevia reads the local storage directory used by the relevant messaging application, strictly limited to temporary status/story files.

No data is transmitted to external servers or stored outside the device.

3.2 Saving downloaded media

Videos and images downloaded through the Application are saved locally and never transmitted to servers.

3.3 Video-to-Audio conversion

The converter extracts the audio track (MP3) from a video file (MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM) already present on the device. Conversion is performed entirely offline, with no upload to any external server.

3.4 Photo to PDF

The Photo to PDF feature assembles one or more photos chosen by the user into a single PDF document. The user selects the photos through the Android system photo picker; Savevia only accesses the specific photos the user selects and has no access to the rest of the user's gallery. The PDF is generated entirely on the device and saved to the device's local storage. No photo and no resulting PDF is uploaded to any server.

3.5 PDF Compression

The PDF Compression feature reduces the file size of a PDF document chosen by the user. The user selects the file through the Android system document picker; Savevia only accesses the specific file the user selects. Compression is performed entirely offline on the device, and the compressed copy is saved to the device's local storage. No PDF file or its content is uploaded to any server.

3.6 Video Compression

The Video Compression feature reduces the file size of a video chosen by the user. The user selects the video through the Android system document picker; Savevia only accesses the specific file the user selects. Processing is performed on the device and the compressed video is saved to the device's local storage. No video file and no part of its content is uploaded to any server, and none is shared with any advertising network.

Article 4 — Vault: Local Secure Storage

The Vault feature allows moving photos, videos, audio and documents into a protected, hidden space within the Application.

4.1 Local-only storage

All Vault files remain stored exclusively in the device's local memory. No Vault content is transmitted to Benashe Softworks or to any third-party server.

4.2 Access protection

Access is protected by a 4-digit PIN code or a password, processed locally via a cryptographic hash, secured via Android Keystore where supported.

4.3 No storage or recovery of the PIN/password by the Studio

  • the Studio cannot recover, reset or bypass a forgotten PIN or password;
  • loss of the PIN/password may result in permanent loss of access to Vault files;
  • the user is solely responsible for remembering their credential and keeping independent backups of important files.

The Vault is a local protection tool, not a backup or cloud storage service.

Article 5 — Security: Protected Content Access Policy

5.1 Systematic blocking of protected content

The Application automatically detects links pointing to protected content (private Stories, locked profiles, private boards, etc.) and blocks their processing.

5.2 Complete absence of login interface

Savevia has no credential entry interface for third-party platforms.

Savevia will NEVER ask for your login credentials on any social media or messaging platform.

Article 6 — No External Data Transmission

  • no personal data stored on remote servers;
  • no data sold or rented, and no personal data transferred to third parties, other than the advertising data processed by Google AdMob as described in Article 7;
  • downloaded or saved media remains on the user's device at all times;
  • Vault files, converter files, photos used for PDF creation, PDF files processed by the compressor and videos processed by the video compressor never leave the user's device;
  • no outgoing network access to personal data, except downloading public media from its original URL, and except the YouTube Summary feature described in Article 10, which transmits only public caption text and only with the user's explicit consent, and the advertising requests described in Article 7.

Article 7 — Advertising

Savevia is entirely free and funded by advertisements. There is no paid or ad-free version of the Application.

7.1 Advertising provider

Advertisements are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it collects through its SDK. Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7.2 Types of ads

  • banner and rectangular ads displayed on some screens of the Application;
  • full-screen (interstitial) ads shown occasionally between two actions, with a limited frequency;
  • a full-screen ad that may appear when you return to the Application after some time away;
  • rewarded video ads, which may be shown before some features are used.

7.3 Data processed by the advertising SDK

To serve, measure and secure advertisements, Google AdMob may collect or use the Android Advertising ID (AAID), the IP address (approximate location), device and operating-system information, and information about your interactions with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

No downloaded media, Vault content, photo, PDF file, video or YouTube text is ever shared with any advertising network.

7.4 Consent and your choices

  • where required by law (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), a consent message is displayed before ads are requested; if you decline, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown;
  • you can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads), reset your Advertising ID, or delete it (Android 12 and later);
  • limiting personalization does not remove ads; it only makes them less targeted.

7.5 Rewarded and full-screen ads

For some features, the Application may offer or ask you to watch a short video ad before the feature starts. If no ad is available, or if you are offline, the feature remains accessible. Ads are shown at a limited frequency, which is calculated locally on the device (see Article 2.4).

7.6 Children

Savevia is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law of their country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided information, contact us at benashesoftworks@gmail.com.

Article 8 — Required Android Permissions

  • INTERNET: downloading media from social platforms and, for the YouTube Summary feature, sending public caption text;
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: checking whether an internet connection is available (downloads, ads and online features);
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): allows Google AdMob to access the Android Advertising ID, as described in Article 7;
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: download, conversion and compression progress notifications;
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (older Android versions only): saving media to the device storage.

Savevia does not request broad access to the user's photos and videos. Photos, videos and PDF files are chosen by the user through the Android system pickers (photo picker and document picker), which grant access only to the specific files selected. Files created by the Application (downloads, converted audio, generated or compressed PDFs) are saved through Android's standard media storage API.

Article 9 — User Rights

The user has rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection regarding data potentially collected by integrated third-party services.

Article 10 — AI-Powered Features: YouTube Video Summary

10.1 What this feature does

Savevia offers an optional feature that generates a written summary and a text transcript of a YouTube video the user chooses, using Google's Gemini API. To use this feature, the user submits a YouTube video URL. The Application retrieves only the video's publicly available text captions/subtitles and sends that text to Google's Gemini API for processing.

No video file, audio file, or any other media byte is ever downloaded, extracted, or transmitted for this feature. Only text (captions/subtitles and the resulting summary) is processed.

10.2 No personal data sent

The only data transmitted to Google for this feature is the YouTube video identifier and its public caption text. Savevia does not send the user's name, device identifiers, account information, or any other personal data as part of this feature.

10.3 Google as a sub-processor

Google, through its Gemini API, acts as a sub-processor for the sole purpose of generating the requested summary. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own terms and privacy practices, independently of Benashe Softworks.

Savevia currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms for this tier, submitted text and generated responses may be used by Google to improve its products and to train and evaluate its models. Users who prefer not to have video caption text processed under these conditions should not use the YouTube Summary feature.

10.4 Data retention

To avoid repeated processing of the same video, Savevia's backend server caches the transcript and summary for up to 30 days. Independently, Google retains its own request logs for this API for a default maximum of 55 days, after which point they are marked for deletion, in accordance with Google's own data retention practices, which are outside Benashe Softworks' control.

10.5 Consent

This feature is not enabled by default and is not required to use the rest of the Application. The first time a user accesses the YouTube Summary feature, the Application presents a clear in-app disclosure of the data practices described in this Article and requires the user's explicit, affirmative consent before any caption text is sent to Google. Declining this consent has no effect on any other feature of the Application.

10.6 AI-generated content accuracy

Summaries are generated automatically by an AI model and may contain inaccuracies, omissions, or errors. See the Terms of Use for details on the limitations of AI-generated content.
