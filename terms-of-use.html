Article 2 — Purpose of the Application

Savevia is an Android application offering the following features:

  • Downloading publicly available videos from popular social media and content-sharing platforms, in multiple formats and qualities;
  • Downloading publicly available images shared on these platforms;
  • Saving temporary status or story content shared via messaging applications, to the device's local storage;

All downloaded, converted, or Vault-protected media is stored exclusively on the user's device local memory.
