Article 2 — Purpose of the Application

Savevia is an Android application offering the following features:

  • Downloading publicly available videos from popular social media and content-sharing platforms, in multiple formats and qualities;
  • Downloading publicly available images shared on these platforms;
  • Saving temporary status or story content shared via messaging applications, to the device's local storage;
  • Converting locally stored video files into audio files (MP3);
  • Converting photos chosen by the user into PDF documents;
  • Compressing PDF files chosen by the user to reduce their size;
  • Compressing video files chosen by the user to reduce their size;
  • Generating an AI-powered written summary and text transcript of a YouTube video chosen by the user;
  • Securely storing personal files in a local, PIN or password-protected Vault.

Downloading videos from YouTube is not supported by the Application, for legal reasons. The YouTube Summary feature processes only the public text captions of a video and never downloads the video itself.

All downloaded, converted, compressed, or Vault-protected media and documents are stored exclusively on the user's device local memory.
